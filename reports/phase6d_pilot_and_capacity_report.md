# Phase 6D pilot and capacity report

The pilot manifest is frozen and contains four deterministic candidates from four chemical clusters. The pilot completed in the validated `mace-lammps` environment using one H800 per candidate. All 16 temperature workflows (300, 400, 500 and 600 K) passed QC, checkpoint/resume passed, and the remaining 124 candidates were not launched. Mean polymer wall time was 1.54 hours and pilot storage was 368.0 MiB. The remaining 124 candidates are estimated at 47.8 wall hours on four GPUs; the full expansion is estimated at 11.5 GiB before safety margin.
