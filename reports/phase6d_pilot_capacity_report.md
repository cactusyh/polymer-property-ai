# Phase 6D pilot capacity report

Mean GPU wall time per polymer was 1.54 hours and pilot storage averaged 92.0 MiB per polymer. Assuming the measured workflow remains stable and four GPUs stay available, the remaining 124 candidates require approximately 47.8 wall hours (2.0 days). The full 128-candidate expansion is estimated at 49.4 wall hours and 11.5 GiB before safety margin.

The pilot observed 37–43% GPU utilization and 668–704 MiB GPU memory per active process. The scheduler remains bounded to one process per GPU and the remaining 124 candidates were not launched.
