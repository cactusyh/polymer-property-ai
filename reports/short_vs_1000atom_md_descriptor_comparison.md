- Local descriptors passing the descriptive stability screen (Spearman >= 0.80; absolute relative mean difference <= 20%): sp2_segment_torsional_deviation_mean.

- Conjugated torsion, ring twist, and sp2 planarity are local by construction, but their paired stability must be interpreted from both this table and the block QC.
- Rg, raw nonlocal pair counts, long-range contact measures, and run-length proxies are chain-scale observables and are expected to depend on chain length unless normalized.
- Future property modelling should use the 1000-atom descriptors as the primary fixed-protocol representation. Short-chain values remain scientifically useful for local torsion/planarity screening only when they show high paired stability; chain-scale short-chain descriptors should not be substituted for the long-chain protocol.

## Direct Answers To The Scientific Questions

1. **Descriptors insensitive to chain length.** Torsional autocorrelation is strongly invariant across the matched set: the 1-frame, 10-ps, and 50-ps autocorrelations change by about -0.3%, -1.2%, and -1.1%, respectively, with Spearman correlations of 0.985, 0.985, and 0.980. The stored sp2 segment torsional-deviation mean changes by 0.13% (Spearman 0.932).

2. **Descriptors that change substantially.** Raw conjugated and aromatic nonlocal pair counts increase by about 1176%, as expected when the chain contains roughly four times as many repeat units. Contact persistence increases by roughly 24-55% depending on cutoff/lag. The aggregate torsion transition-rate fields increase by about 261-263%; because their stored estimator aggregates transitions over all mapped torsions, this is a chain-size-sensitive count-density measure rather than a length-invariant local rate.

3. **Are conjugated torsion, ring twist, and sp2 planarity mainly local?** The five-block long-chain QC supports local temporal stability: median CV is 0.29% for conjugated-torsion mean, 0.15% for planar fraction, and about 1.2-1.3% for ring-plane RMSD/out-of-plane displacement. Ring twist is less stable but remains local in definition (median CV 4.83%). Direct short-versus-long paired statistics for torsion mean, planar fraction, and ring twist are unavailable because the supplied short-chain parquet does not store those fields under the same schema; therefore this analysis does not claim a numerical cross-length equivalence for them.

4. **Are Rg, long-range contacts, and conjugation-run descriptors chain-length dependent?** Yes for Rg and raw nonlocal contact/pair descriptors by physical construction. Rg and Re/Rg cannot be directly compared here because the short-chain supplied parquet does not contain compatible stored values. Longest-planar-run and soft-conjugation-length proxy are likewise absent from the supplied long-chain schema, so no valid paired numerical claim is made.

5. **Future property-modelling representation.** Use the 1000-atom descriptor protocol as the primary fixed-chain-length representation. It avoids conflating molecular size with local and nonlocal observables. This analysis does not train, fit, or evaluate any band-gap model.

6. **Scientifically usable short-chain descriptors.** Short-chain torsional autocorrelations and the shared sp2 segment torsional-deviation statistic remain useful for comparative local screening because their matched correlations are high and mean shifts are small. Short-chain raw pair counts, contact persistence, aggregate transition counts/rates, Rg, and any chain-scale proxy should only be used with explicit chain-length normalization or a fixed chain-length protocol.

## Comparability Caveat

The requested tables are matched exactly by the 200 polymer identities. However, not every focus descriptor is present under the same column name and aggregation schema in both supplied parquet files. The CSV therefore reports all directly shared numeric descriptors with paired N, means, differences, and correlations, and the report explicitly flags unavailable direct comparisons rather than imputing or redefining descriptors.
